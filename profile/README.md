# Twitch - Game Streaming Platform

> **Your live streaming workspace: watch channels, join chat, and broadcast gameplay with one clear Twitch experience.**

## About Twitch

Twitch is a live streaming platform built for gamers, creators, and communities who want real-time video and chat in one place. Twitch game streaming keeps broadcasts readable and interactive, so viewers can follow matches, watch speedruns, and join conversations without leaving the page. With Twitch live channels, people discover new creators, follow favorites, and return to ongoing series. Twitch stream setup helps new broadcasters configure scenes, audio, and alerts before going live. Twitch viewer interaction turns passive watching into active participation through chat, emotes, and channel points. Twitch broadcaster tools support streamers who want to grow an audience, schedule shows, and review analytics after each session. Whether you are here to watch or to broadcast, Twitch keeps the path from first click to live moment simple and steady.
